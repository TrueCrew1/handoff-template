How the system works

Five connected steps form a repeatable shift workflow. Complete each section in order during the shift; data flows into your end-of-shift passdown automatically.

  • 01 First 15 Minutes — Orient the incoming crew on scope, risk, and priorities.
  • 02 Shift-Resilient Job Plan — Document active work so it survives crew changes.
  • 03 Repeat Failure Log — Track asset failures and flag repeats within 30 days.
  • 04 Handoff Readiness Scorecard — Confirm the shift is ready before handoff.
  • 05 Generate Shift Passdown — Compile everything into one handoff document.
  1. Start of shift — Complete the First 15 Minutes orientation. Confirm isolations, inspect priority assets, and review open job packs.
  2. During shift — Record Shift-Resilient Job Plans for active work. Log failures by asset ID; repeat events are flagged automatically.
  3. Before handoff — Complete the Handoff Readiness Scorecard. All required items, including inspection or justified N/A, must be marked complete.
  4. End of shift — Supervisor name and shift are pre-filled from Section 01. Generate the passdown, then print or copy for the incoming crew.
